# Northstar Gear Refund FAQ

## When money is returned

- Card refunds post **5–10 business days** after the warehouse accepts the return.
- The refund goes to the original payment method. We cannot send it to a different card.
- Gift-card purchases are refunded as store credit on a Northstar Gear gift card.

## Cancel vs refund

- **Cancel** is for orders that have not been delivered yet, or for delivered orders still inside the 30-day return window that the customer no longer wants.
- **Refund** is the money movement after a cancel or an accepted return.
- An order that is **shipped but not delivered** cannot be refunded yet. The customer can wait for delivery and return it, or ask a human to attempt a carrier intercept.

## Partial refunds

- If only one item from a multi-item order is returned, we refund that item plus the unused portion of shipping only when the entire order is sent back.
- Shipping is not refunded on partial returns.

## Chargebacks and disputes

- If a customer mentions a **chargeback**, bank dispute, or fraud investigation, **do not** process a cancel or refund in the bot.
- Escalate immediately to a human specialist. Dual refunds (bot refund + chargeback) create accounting errors.

## Promotions

- If an item was bought with a site-wide discount, the refund is the amount actually paid, not the list price.
- Free-gift promotions: the gift must be returned unused or its value is deducted from the refund.
