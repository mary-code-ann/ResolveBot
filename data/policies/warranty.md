# Northstar Gear Limited Warranty

## Coverage

Northstar Gear warrants manufacturing defects for **1 year from the delivery date**.

Covered examples:

- Broken zippers that were not forced
- Separating seams
- Hardware that failed under normal outdoor use

Not covered:

- Normal wear (fading, pilling, stretched elastic)
- Damage from misuse, alterations, or accidents
- Lost items

## How a warranty claim works

1. The customer provides the order ID and a short description of the defect.
2. A human warranty desk reviews photos. ResolveBot cannot approve a warranty replacement.
3. If approved, we repair, replace, or refund the item at our option.

## Warranty vs return

- Inside 30 days, prefer the return policy when the customer simply changed their mind.
- After 30 days, only manufacturing defects qualify, and those must be escalated to the warranty desk.

## Proof of purchase

A valid order ID (ORD-####) or a receipt photo is required. Without proof of purchase, escalate to a human.
