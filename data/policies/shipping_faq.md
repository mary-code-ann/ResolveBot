# Northstar Gear Shipping FAQ

## Processing time

- In-stock orders are packed within **1–2 business days**.
- Preorder or backordered items ship when inventory arrives. The product page shows the estimated ship week.

## Carriers and transit

- Standard shipping: 3–7 business days after the label is created. Carrier: Northstar Logistics or USPS.
- Expedited shipping: 2 business days after the label is created.
- Tracking appears in the order record once the label is scanned.

## Delivery status meanings

- **processing**: payment captured, not yet packed
- **shipped**: label created and the carton left the warehouse
- **delivered**: carrier marked the package delivered
- **cancelled**: the order was stopped before or after fulfillment

## Missing or late packages

- Wait **24 hours** after a "delivered" scan before reporting a missing package. Many deliveries are left with a neighbor or in a locker.
- If a package is still missing after 24 hours, escalate to a human agent so they can open a carrier claim.
- ResolveBot can share tracking and status. It cannot file a carrier claim.

## Address changes

- Address can be updated only while the order is **processing**.
- After the order is **shipped**, we cannot redirect it. The customer can refuse the delivery or start a return after it arrives.

## International

This demo policy covers U.S. addresses only. International shipping questions should be escalated to a human.
