# Northstar Gear Return Policy

Company: Northstar Gear  
Applies to: online and retail purchases in the United States

## Return window

You may return unused or lightly used items within **30 days of delivery**.

- The 30-day clock starts on the delivery date, not the order date.
- After 30 days, standard returns are closed. Manufacturing defects may still qualify under the warranty policy.

## Condition rules

- **Unopened items** in original packaging: full refund of the item price. No restocking fee.
- **Opened but unused items** with tags attached: full refund if the item can be resold as new.
- **Used items** (signs of wear, dirt, or missing tags): not eligible for a standard return. A store credit of 50% may be offered at agent discretion after photos are reviewed by a human.
- **Final-sale items** (marked "Final Sale" on the product page): cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.

## How to start a return

1. Have your order ID ready (format: ORD-1000).
2. Ask ResolveBot to look up the order, or email returns@northstargear.example.
3. If the order is still within 30 days and eligible, we issue a prepaid return label.
4. Refunds are processed after the warehouse scans the return, not when the label is created.

## What is not returnable

- Gift cards
- Worn footwear with creased soles (hygiene)
- Custom-embroidered gear
- Items reported lost after the customer already received a replacement

## Restocking

A **15% restocking fee** applies only when an opened item is returned without original packaging. Unopened boxed items have no restocking fee.
